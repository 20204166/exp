# Enforcement Pack

Primary reviewer: Reviewer 5 — Enforcement & Consequence Reviewer.

Use this pack to evaluate consequences after authority, applicability, repository evidence, and operational evidence have been reviewed. It does not reinterpret legislation.

## Owned question

```text
If the earlier validated evidence is correct, what are the likely harms, regulatory routes, severity, likelihood, and commercial or civil consequences?
```

## Hard boundary

Reviewer 5 must not reopen legal authority, commencement, amendments, applicability, repo evidence, or deployment proof. If earlier evidence is weak, say so and cap consequence confidence. Serious harm does not convert weak legal proof into a proven non-compliance finding.

## Inputs

Reviewer 5 reads:

- verified authority record from Reviewer 1;
- applicability record from Reviewer 2;
- repository evidence record from Reviewer 3;
- operational evidence record from Reviewer 4;
- initial finding and confidence dimensions.

## Consequence dimensions

Assess only dimensions relevant to the scoped finding:

- consumer/passenger/data-subject harm;
- financial harm, delay, inconvenience, denial of rights, distress, discrimination, unfairness, or loss of trust;
- likelihood and scale of affected users;
- detectability by user, operator, regulator, or third party;
- remediation difficulty and urgency;
- complaint, chargeback, ombudsman, civil claim, or contractual route;
- regulator interest and possible enforcement route;
- commercial, operational, and reputational impact;
- historic exposure versus prospective risk;
- whether qualified legal review is required.

## Regulator consequence framing

Use regulator-specific consequence framing only where the earlier reviewer chain established remit/applicability. Possible bodies include ICO, FCA, CMA, Trading Standards, ASA, ORR, CAA, Ofcom, HMRC, NCSC, Companies House, and other competent UK regulators.

Use official enforcement decisions, notices, regulator policy, statutory guidance, and official reports before secondary commentary. Record if no official enforcement comparator was found and the search limitations.

## Severity and likelihood

Record severity and likelihood separately:

```text
Severity: low / medium / high / critical / unresolved
Likelihood: low / medium / high / unknown
Basis:
Evidence dependencies:
Confidence cap:
```

Do not inflate severity because a fix is easy. Do not downgrade severity because evidence is incomplete; instead cap confidence and use an evidence-gap status.

## High-risk escalation signals

Escalate for qualified legal review where the finding involves any high-risk trigger from `SKILL.md`, including significant consumer detriment, financial services/payments, special-category data, children, discrimination, cross-border processing, regulatory notification, material civil liability, threatened litigation, unclear territorial scope, disputed legal role, or competing legal interpretations.
